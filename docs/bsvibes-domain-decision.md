## Key reasoning by lane

- **Sales/Brand**: "Vibes" invites you into a scene; "vibe" hands you a business card
- **Design**: "BSVIBES" has better typographic rhythm; "BSVIBE" looks truncated
- **Research**: "Vibes" dominates crypto Twitter/Discord culture; plural SEO captures both forms
- **Community**: BSV crowd is culture-forward — "vibes" reads as insider fluency
- **Docs**: "Feel the BSVibes" works; "Feel the BSVibe" sounds unfinished
- **AI/Naming**: LLMs will parse "BSVibes" cleanly; "BSVibe" risks being read as "BS Vibe"
- **Strategy**: Plural names scale into platforms; singular names get confused with personal brands

## The dissent (Torque)

One character matters on mobile — 10 chars vs 11. Shorter domains have higher unaided recall after 48 hours. If you ever run paid ads where users type the URL cold, A/B test this. It's a real but narrow concern that doesn't outweigh the rest.

## Verdict

**bsvibes.com — high confidence.** The semantic, social, and cultural advantages compound in exactly the ways that drive network effects. Register it and move on.
